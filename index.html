Reading this dashboard

Status & data legend

Equipment type

Type labels and restrained tile accents distinguish incubators, freezers, and refrigerators.

Operational status

Plain-language label and icon indicate a manually maintained status. Unknown means the source did not provide a confirmed current status.

Monitoring status

Shown separately from operational status. It is not a live REES connection or measurement.

Last updated

The most recent populated record review date. Blank dates are shown as “Not provided.”

Setpoints are targets

Temperature and humidity are target conditions from the equipment register, not current measured readings.
